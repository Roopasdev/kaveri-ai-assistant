# Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Kaveri Exports. Owner: Human Resources.

## Your first week

You receive your laptop and ID card on day one, meet your buddy, and complete the IT security and code of conduct training.

## Probation

New employees serve a probation period of 6 months. Your manager reviews your work at the end of it and confirms your role.

## Getting help

Ask your buddy, your manager or HR. The HR portal has every policy.
