# Remote Work Policy

Kaveri Exports Private Limited. Owner: Human Resources.

## Who can work from home

Employees in office roles may work from home up to 1 day(s) a week, agreed with their manager. Warehouse and quality roles work on site.

## Core hours

Everyone is available between 10:00 to 16:00 IST, wherever they work.

## Equipment

Use the company laptop and a secure home network. Public Wi-Fi needs the VPN.
