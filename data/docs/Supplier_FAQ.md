# Supplier FAQ

Answers for farmers and suppliers who sell to Kaveri Exports. Owner: Procurement.

## When are suppliers paid?

Suppliers are paid within 45 days of the goods passing quality inspection.

## How do I register as a supplier?

Fill the supplier form at the Coimbatore office with your GST number and bank details. Procurement visits the farm or unit before the first order.
